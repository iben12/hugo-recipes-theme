Lemon and herb roast chicken

A golden, lemony Sunday roast with crisp potatoes and plenty of fresh herbs.

Szerző: The Sunday Table

Elkészítés
20 minutes
Főzési idő
1 hour 15 minutes
Adag
4

The kind of relaxed, generous roast that fills the kitchen with the smell of lemon and rosemary. Put the potatoes in the same pan and let the oven do the hard work.

Mi kell hozzá

Hozzávalók

  • 1 whole chicken, about 1.6 kg
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 4 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 700 g waxy potatoes, cut into chunks
  • A small handful each of rosemary and thyme
  • Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper

Kezdjünk főzni

Elékészítés

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F. Pat the chicken dry and season it generously inside and out.
  2. Put half the herbs, the lemon halves, and garlic inside the cavity. Rub the skin with olive oil and scatter the remaining herbs over the chicken and potatoes.
  3. Arrange the potatoes in a roasting pan and sit the chicken on top. Roast for 1 hour 15 minutes, turning the potatoes once, until the chicken is deeply golden and cooked through.
  4. Rest the chicken for 15 minutes before carving. Serve with the crisp potatoes and pan juices.
