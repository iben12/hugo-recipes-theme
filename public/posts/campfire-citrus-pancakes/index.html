Campfire citrus pancakes

Fluffy, bright pancakes for slow mornings and outdoor breakfasts.

Szerző: The Sunday Table

Elkészítés
10 minutes
Főzési idő
20 minutes
Adag
4

Orange zest gives these tender pancakes a little lift. They're equally good made at home or cooked outside on a cool morning.

Mi kell hozzá

Hozzávalók

  • 200 g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250 ml milk
  • 1 large egg
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • 2 tbsp melted butter, plus more for the pan
  • Maple syrup and seasonal berries, to serve

Kezdjünk főzni

Elékészítés

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, and baking powder together in a bowl.

  2. In a second bowl, whisk the milk, egg, orange zest, and melted butter. Stir into the dry ingredients just until combined; a few small lumps are fine.

  3. Warm a lightly buttered skillet over medium heat. Cook small ladlefuls of batter until bubbles appear on top, then flip and cook until golden.

  4. Serve warm with maple syrup and berries.

„A szemétől a szájig, valami sós folyik és ő csak birkózik az ízzel...”.
