Slow-roasted tomato pasta

Jammy tomatoes, garlic, and a little pasta water make a weeknight sauce worth lingering over.

Szerző: The Sunday Table

Elkészítés
10 minutes
Főzési idő
45 minutes
Adag
4

Roasting coaxes out the tomatoes' sweetness and makes a rich sauce with almost no hands-on time. A few torn basil leaves bring everything to life.

Mi kell hozzá

Hozzávalók

  • 800 g ripe cherry tomatoes
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 400 g rigatoni or other short pasta
  • A pinch of chilli flakes
  • A handful of fresh basil leaves
  • 50 g finely grated Parmesan, plus more to serve
  • Sea salt and black pepper

Kezdjünk főzni

Elékészítés

  1. Heat the oven to 170°C / 340°F. Put the tomatoes and garlic in a baking dish, drizzle with olive oil, and season with salt, pepper, and chilli flakes.

  2. Roast for 40 to 45 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed and their juices are syrupy.

Tipp

If your tomatoes are on the large side, halve them before roasting so they soften evenly.

  1. Cook the pasta in well-salted water until just al dente. Save a mug of pasta water before draining.

  2. Fold the pasta into the tomatoes with the Parmesan and a splash of pasta water. Toss until glossy, then finish with basil and more Parmesan.
