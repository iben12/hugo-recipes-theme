prepTime = '10 minutes'
cookTime = '45 minutes'
servings = '4'
ingredients = [
  '800 g ripe cherry tomatoes',
  '4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced',
  '4 tbsp olive oil',
  '400 g rigatoni or other short pasta',
  'A pinch of chilli flakes',
  'A handful of fresh basil leaves',
  '50 g finely grated Parmesan, plus more to serve',
  'Sea salt and black pepper',
]
+++

1. Heat the oven to 170°C / 340°F. Put the tomatoes and garlic in a baking dish, drizzle with olive oil, and season with salt, pepper, and chilli flakes.

2. Roast for 40 to 45 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed and their juices are syrupy.

{{< tip >}}
If your tomatoes are on the large side, halve them before roasting so they soften evenly.
{{< /tip >}}

3. Cook the pasta in well-salted water until just al dente. Save a mug of pasta water before draining.

4. Fold the pasta into the tomatoes with the Parmesan and a splash of pasta water. Toss until glossy, then finish with basil and more Parmesan.
